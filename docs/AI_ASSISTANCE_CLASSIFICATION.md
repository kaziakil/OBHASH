# OBHASH AI Assistance Classification Framework

## 1. Classification Philosophy: Agency, Not Avoidance

In conventional education and workplace platforms, AI usage is frequently treated as cheating, leading to adversarial cat-and-mouse detection software. In contrast, OBHASH’s Human-Centered Intelligence philosophy asserts:

> **AI is an intellectual collaborator. The critical distinction is not *whether* AI was touched, but *how* it was engaged: did the human lead the reasoning and audit the output, or did the human passively outsource their thinking?**

To operationalize this distinction without resorting to unscientific AI-detection tools, OBHASH establishes a **5-Tier AI Assistance Classification Engine**.

---

## 2. The 5 Assistance Tiers

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        AI ASSISTANCE SPECTRUM                          │
├─────────────────┬──────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┤
│    Level A      │     Level B      │     Level C     │     Level D     │
│   Independent   │    AI-Informed   │   AI-Assisted   │   AI-Generated  │
│  (Zero queries) │ (Clarification)  │ (Collaborative) │ (Substantial AI)│
└─────────────────┴──────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┘
                                                       │
                                                       ▼
                                                    Level E
                                                    Unknown
                                            (Ambiguous telemetry)
```

### Level A — Independent (Wholly Self-Authored)
- **Definition:** The learner developed, structured, drafted, and submitted the deliverable without recorded AI assistance or coaching sessions.
- **Criteria:** 0 coaching queries recorded; 0 hint levels requested; or user explicit disclosure of independent formulation.
- **Interpretation:** Demonstrates unassisted cognitive fluency in this specific task context.

### Level B — AI-Informed (Background Exploration & Clarification)
- **Definition:** The learner engaged AI to look up definitions, clarify confusing technical terms, explore historical precedents, or summarize background concepts. However, all core arguments, hypotheses, and trade-off decisions were independently generated by the learner.
- **Criteria:** 1–2 queries recorded; hint level $\le 1$; query actions restricted to `clarify_definition`, `orient`, or background explanation.
- **Interpretation:** Exemplary use of AI as an informational reference library while maintaining complete intellectual leadership.

### Level C — AI-Assisted (Collaborative Thinking Partner)
- **Definition:** The AI provided structured scaffolding, suggested divergent angles, or offered constructive challenges (Levels 2–3 hints) which the learner critically examined, filtered, and synthesized into their own work.
- **Criteria:** 3–6 queries recorded; progressive hint levels 2–3 utilized; assistance summary notes human critique and adaptation.
- **Interpretation:** Strong demonstration of Socratic partnership; the learner utilizes AI to push beyond availability heuristics while retaining editorial authority.

### Level D — AI-Generated (Substantial AI Authorship)
- **Definition:** AI generated major portions of the submitted solution, and the human learner accepted the output without sufficient evidence of independent critique, verification, or material adaptation.
- **Criteria:** 8+ queries; hint level 4 (direct solution); user submitted text closely mirroring raw AI responses.
- **Interpretation:** High dependence observed. Independent reasoning cannot be certified for this deliverable.

### Level E — Unknown (Ambiguous Telemetry / Unclassified)
- **Definition:** Telemetry data is incomplete, the work was drafted in an external unmonitored environment, or conflicting indicators exist.
- **Criteria:** Missing telemetry; unresolved user dispute; or imported legacy data.
- **Interpretation:** OBHASH strictly marks uncertain cases as **Unknown** to prevent false claims or unfair accusations.

---

## 3. Strict Rules & Safeguards

1. **No Stylometric AI Detectors:** The system never employs probabilistic AI detectors (e.g. perplexity/burstiness scanners) as proof of AI authorship, as such tools are widely documented to exhibit high false-positive rates on non-native English speakers.
2. **Honoring User Disclosures:** If a learner provides an explicit, reasoned disclosure explaining how AI was utilized, the system prioritizes the disclosure over automated telemetry.
3. **No Character Judgment:** Classifications describe specific activity deliverables, not a person's character, morality, or general capability.
