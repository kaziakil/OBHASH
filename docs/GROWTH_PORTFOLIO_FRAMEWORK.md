# OBHASH Growth Portfolio Framework

## 1. Overview and Core Purpose

The **Growth Portfolio** (`/portfolio` and `/p/[publicSlug]`) transforms verified capability evidence into a dignified, user-controlled representation of personal development.

Unlike conventional social networks, academic transcripts, or resume websites:
- It does not display vanity metrics, follow counts, or standardized test percentiles.
- It is **private by default**.
- It highlights authentic problem-solving artifacts, real-world constraints, metacognitive reflections, and transparent AI assistance disclosures.

---

## 2. Six Structured Portfolio Sections

| Section Type | Section Title | Purpose & User Control |
|---|---|---|
| `personal_intro` | Personal Introduction & Learning Philosophy | Learner-authored statement of goals, areas of interest, and stance on human agency in the age of AI. |
| `capability_overview` | Demonstrated Capabilities | Evidence-backed summary of demonstrated capabilities (Critical Thinking, Creative Problem-Solving, Curiosity). |
| `featured_work` | Featured Challenge Work | Curated challenge deliverables, solutions to real-world friction, and problem-solving artifacts. |
| `development_journey` | Growth Journey & Milestones | Highlights from personalized 8-week developmental pathways and completed milestones. |
| `thoughtful_ai_use` | Thoughtful AI Use Disclosures | Explicit declarations and examples of how the learner partners with AI while maintaining critical agency. |
| `personal_reflections` | Personal Metacognitive Reflections | Candid reflections on cognitive heuristics, mental biases overcome, and shifts in perspective. |

---

## 3. Evidence Selection & Editorial Integrity

1. **User Ownership:** Learners select which specific completed challenges or assessments to feature using the interactive modal picker.
2. **Provenance Preservation:** When an item is added to the portfolio, its original source reference (`sourceRecordId`), capability tag, and creation date remain permanently attached.
3. **Mandatory AI Disclosure:** If a featured piece of work utilized AI assistance, the corresponding classification badge (`Level A` through `Level E`) and disclosure note are prominently rendered. Learners cannot strip AI disclosures to present AI-assisted work as unassisted.
4. **Draft vs. Published State:** Modifications remain in draft mode until the learner clicks **Save Draft** or toggles publication.
5. **Print & PDF Export:** The showcase includes print-ready CSS (`@media print`) and an **Export / Print PDF** trigger allowing learners to generate clean PDF portfolio documents for mentors, employers, or academic applications.
