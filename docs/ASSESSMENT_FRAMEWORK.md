# OBHASH Capability Assessment Framework

**Phase:** 3 of 8 (Capability Assessment Engine)  
**Philosophy:** Human-Centered Intelligence  
**Target Audience:** Ages 18–30  

---

## 1. Assessment Philosophy

The OBHASH Capability Assessment Engine is built on the premise that genuine human capabilities must be evaluated through **demonstrated performance** rather than subjective self-reporting.

### Core Principles

1. **Assess Demonstrated Capabilities**: The system presents realistic scenarios, multi-perspective data, and constrained challenges where users actively exhibit their reasoning, creativity, and inquiry.
2. **Measure Without Labeling**: Results describe observed performance within specific tasks. The engine never assigns fixed personality labels (e.g. "uncreative", "poor thinker").
3. **Prioritize Independent Performance**: Assessments are completed without AI assistance inside OBHASH. The AI Growth Coach is intentionally disabled during assessments to establish a true human baseline.
4. **Transparent Evaluation**: Users can inspect the exact criteria, 4-level rubrics, and cited excerpts from their own responses that justified each score.
5. **Avoid False Precision**: Results are explicitly communicated as internal OBHASH performance indicators, not standardized IQ scores or psychometric population percentiles.

### 2.1 Critical Thinking
Evaluating claims, distinguishing evidence from unstated assumptions, detecting cognitive biases, and formulating defensible judgments.

### 2.2 Creative Problem-Solving
Divergent thinking, synthesizing non-obvious connections, reframing severe constraints, and iteratively improving flawed systems.

### 2.3 Curiosity
Formulating deep, mechanistic questions, investigating controversial claims with scientific literacy, and discovering unexpected interdisciplinary connections.

## 3. Four-Level Scoring Framework

Each assessment dimension is evaluated on a strict 1 to 4 integer scale:

| Level | Performance Tier | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Level 1** | **Emerging** | Limited or unevidenced application of the intended capability; relies on unsupported assertions or conventional clichés. |
| **Level 2** | **Developing** | Some relevant capability demonstrated, but with notable evidential gaps, superficial critique, or unaddressed constraints. |
| **Level 3** | **Demonstrating** | Clear, sound evidence of capability; separates claims from evidence, proposes viable alternatives, and asks generative questions. |
| **Level 4** | **Strongly Demonstrating** | Consistent, nuanced, multi-perspective reasoning; synthesizes complex trade-offs, anticipates edge cases, and exhibits profound inquiry. |

---

## 4. Evaluation Limitations & Scientific Boundary

- **Prototype-Level Indicators**: The initial OBHASH assessments are educational capability indicators designed to guide personal growth.
- **Not a Clinical Diagnosis**: They are not formal psychometric instruments, clinical evaluations, or standardized employment tests.
- **Language Confounding**: While designed for clear English with planned Bangla adaptation, language fluency can impact written expression depth. Evaluators are tuned to score underlying logic rather than grammatical sophistication.
