## Module 1: Critical Thinking (`critical-thinking`)
- **Estimated Duration**: 8–10 minutes
- **Dimensions Evaluated**: Analytical Reasoning, Evidence Evaluation, Logical Reasoning, Perspective Evaluation, Judgment

### Activity 1: Evaluate an Argument
- **Activity Type**: `argument-evaluation`
- **Dimension**: Evidence Evaluation
- **Format**: Structured Reasoning (4 sub-prompts)
- **Scenario**: Corporate memo claiming a 25% patent surge proves remote work suppresses innovation.
- **Key Focus**: Identifying correlation vs causation, voluntary survey bias, and missing counter-evidence.

### Activity 2: Analyze Conflicting Information
- **Activity Type**: `conflicting-sources`
- **Dimension**: Perspective Evaluation
- **Format**: Structured Reasoning (3 sub-prompts)
- **Scenario**: Source A (Municipal Transit: emissions dropped 14%) vs Source B (Pedestrian Safety: hospital admissions rose 68%).
- **Key Focus**: Analyzing selective statistical framing, base-rate fallacies, and defining empirical verification criteria.

### Activity 3: Reasoning Under Uncertainty
- **Activity Type**: `reasoning-scenario`
- **Dimension**: Judgment
- **Format**: Long-text reasoning response
- **Scenario**: $1,000,000 non-profit grant allocation dilemma: Direct Cohort Expansion (250 students, 82% employment) vs Open Learning Platform (10,000 users, 6% completion).
- **Key Focus**: Balancing depth vs scale, systemic risk hedging, and milestone gating.

## Module 2: Creative Problem-Solving (`creative-problem-solving`)
- **Estimated Duration**: 8–10 minutes
- **Dimensions Evaluated**: Idea Generation, Originality, Flexibility, Practical Reasoning, Iterative Thinking

### Activity 1: Generate Possibilities
- **Activity Type**: `possibility-generation`
- **Dimension**: Originality & Idea Generation
- **Format**: Structured Reasoning (3 sub-prompts)
- **Scenario**: 35% of student grocery food spoils due to erratic schedules and shared cramped fridges.
- **Key Focus**: Divergent brainstorming across multiple paradigms, detailing an unconventional solution, and resolving social friction.

### Activity 2: Solve Under Severe Constraints
- **Activity Type**: `constraint-solving`
- **Dimension**: Practical Reasoning
- **Format**: Long-text reasoning response
- **Scenario**: 10-day campus power & cellular blackout; 1,200 students across 4km radius; zero power, zero fuel, under $100 budget.
- **Key Focus**: Designing an asynchronous human mesh communication network using everyday physical items.

### Activity 3: Improve an Existing Solution
- **Activity Type**: `solution-improvement`
- **Dimension**: Iterative Thinking
- **Format**: Structured Reasoning (3 sub-prompts)
- **Scenario**: Peer-mentorship platform matching students by major/hobbies suffers an 82% in-person meeting drop-off.
- **Key Focus**: Diagnosing social awkwardness and coordination friction; redesigning around low-stakes micro-collaborations.

## Module 3: Curiosity (`curiosity`)
- **Estimated Duration**: 8–10 minutes
- **Dimensions Evaluated**: Question Formulation, Exploration, Information Seeking, Intellectual Openness, Investigative Reasoning

### Activity 1: Question the Unknown
- **Activity Type**: `questioning-phenomenon`
- **Dimension**: Question Formulation
- **Format**: Structured Reasoning (3 sub-prompts)
- **Scenario**: Hydrothermal vent worm colony pulsing bioluminescent waves every evening at 21:00 UTC in pitch-black depths.
- **Key Focus**: Formulating deep mechanistic questions, contrasting with superficial queries, and designing a controlled experiment.

### Activity 2: Investigate an Extraordinary Claim
- **Activity Type**: `claim-investigation`
- **Dimension**: Intellectual Openness & Claim Investigation
- **Format**: Structured Reasoning (3 sub-prompts)
- **Scenario**: Viral claim that a $299 40Hz acoustic headband increases problem-solving by 45%.
- **Key Focus**: Demanding sham-controlled double-blind trials, checking startup funding conflicts, and specifying falsification thresholds.

### Activity 3: Explore Unexpected Connections
- **Activity Type**: `connecting-concepts`
- **Dimension**: Cross-Disciplinary Exploration
- **Format**: Long-text reasoning response
- **Scenario**: Intersecting fungal mycelium distribution networks with decentralized peer-to-peer cloud protocols.
- **Key Focus**: Finding non-obvious algorithmic parallels, Byzantine fault analogies, and proposing an innovative cross-domain application.
