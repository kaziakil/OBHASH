# OBHASH Challenge Framework

**Phase:** Phase 6 of 8 (Challenge & Reflection Workspace)  
**Status:** Complete & Active  
**Philosophy:** Human-Centered Intelligence

---

## 1. Challenge Design Principles

OBHASH challenges are not quizzes, memory tests, or multiple-choice questions. They are structured, open-ended scenarios designed to stimulate authentic human reasoning.

Every challenge adheres to five core principles:
1. **Real-World Fidelity**: Scenarios reflect genuine dilemmas in science, technology, public policy, management, and everyday life.
2. **Multi-Approach Viability**: There is no single "correct" answer; excellence is measured by the quality, coherence, and evidentiary depth of the user's reasoning.
3. **Intellectual Scaffolding Without Hand-Holding**: Clear scenario briefings and suggested thinking steps provide orientation without giving away solutions.
4. **Cognitive Friction by Design**: Challenges intentionally introduce competing constraints, missing data, or conflicting perspectives to encourage deep thought.
5. **Human-Centered Agency**: The AI Growth Coach assists with questioning and heuristics, but the learner remains the sole author of their decisions and deliverables.

---

## 2. Difficulty Levels

| Level | Characteristics | Target Learner State |
|---|---|---|
| **Beginner** | Accessible, familiar scenarios; structured prompts; clear causal links; foundational cognitive models. | Building confidence and learning to articulate premises and assumptions explicitly. |
| **Intermediate** | Competing stakeholder claims; ambiguous data; trade-off evaluation; multi-paradigm solution architectures. | Developing cognitive flexibility, perspective evaluation, and practical feasibility reasoning. |
| **Advanced** | Severe resource constraints; wicked problem environments; interdisciplinary analogical leaps; empirical boundary audits. | Demonstrating intellectual openness, high-level synthesis, and independent critical judgment. |

## 3. Capability Categories & Catalog Roster

### 3.1 Critical Thinking
Focuses on evaluating claims, examining evidence, identifying assumptions, analyzing arguments, and making reasoned judgments.

1. **The Unproven Claim: Dissecting Wellness Tech Assertions** (`ct-unproven-claim`, Beginner, 20-25 min)
   - *Dimensions:* Analytical Reasoning, Evidence Evaluation, Logical Reasoning.
   - *Scenario:* A startup claims a micro-vibrational headband boosts focus by 42% based on a 30-person pilot paired with daily meditation.
   - *Deliverables:* Deconstructed claims, hidden assumptions/confounders, minimum empirical standard of proof.

2. **Two Sides of the Story: Workplace Productivity Tracking** (`ct-two-sides`, Intermediate, 30-35 min)
   - *Dimensions:* Perspective Evaluation, Analytical Reasoning, Judgment.
   - *Scenario:* Enterprise management demands continuous biometric and keystroke tracking; engineering council objects citing Goodhart's law and psychological safety.
   - *Deliverables:* Comparative critique, core value trade-offs, reasoned synthetic policy.

3. **The Missing Evidence: Urban Micro-Mobility Ban** (`ct-missing-evidence`, Beginner, 15-20 min)
   - *Dimensions:* Evidence Evaluation, Judgment, Logical Reasoning.
   - *Scenario:* A city bans dockless e-scooters after 14 emergency room visits without comparative rate or baseline transport data.
   - *Deliverables:* Critical data voids, plausible alternative hypotheses.

4. **The Causal Trap: Remote Work & Innovation Velocity** (`ct-causal-trap`, Intermediate, 25-30 min)
   - *Dimensions:* Analytical Reasoning, Logical Reasoning, Evidence Evaluation.
   - *Scenario:* A conglomerate claims remote work caused a 22% drop in patent filings, ignoring concurrent 15% R&D budget cuts and executive restructuring.
   - *Deliverables:* Causal critique, confounding variable matrix, rigorous study design.

---

### 3.2 Creative Problem-Solving
Focuses on divergent ideation, working under extreme constraints, redesigning ordinary objects, and cross-domain analogical synthesis.

5. **Solve It Differently: Combating Campus Food Waste** (`cps-solve-differently`, Beginner, 20-25 min)
   - *Dimensions:* Idea Generation, Originality, Cognitive Flexibility.
   - *Scenario:* University dining halls discard 800kg of edible food daily due to oversized buffet portions and culinary over-preparation.
   - *Deliverables:* Three radically divergent concepts (Behavioral Nudge, Predictive Logistics, Incentive Restructuring), friction analysis.

6. **The Constraint Challenge: Zero-Electricity Vaccine Cold Chain** (`cps-constraint-challenge`, Intermediate, 25-30 min)
   - *Dimensions:* Practical Reasoning, Originality, Iterative Thinking.
   - *Scenario:* Off-grid rural healthcare transit in 35°C tropical heat; zero electricity; budget <$15; weight <5kg; duration >8 hours.
   - *Deliverables:* Passive thermodynamic cooling mechanism, constraint verification, failure-mode mitigation.

7. **Improve the Ordinary: Reimagining the Public Transit Turnstile** (`cps-improve-ordinary`, Beginner, 15-20 min)
   - *Dimensions:* Practical Reasoning, Originality, Cognitive Flexibility.
   - *Scenario:* Mechanical turnstiles create commuter bottlenecks, bag snags, wheelchair exclusion, and anxiety during peak rush hours.
   - *Deliverables:* Friction points analysis, redesigned entry experience, universal accessibility assessment.

8. **The Analogical Leap: Biomimetic Urban Heat Defense** (`cps-analogical-leap`, Advanced, 35-40 min)
   - *Dimensions:* Originality, Idea Generation, Cognitive Flexibility.
   - *Scenario:* Downtown masonry and asphalt create nocturnal heat islands 7°C hotter than rural surroundings; traditional HVAC worsens street heat.
   - *Deliverables:* Biological source adaptation, architectural translation, systemic block integration.

### 3.3 Curiosity
Focuses on asking deeper questions, uncovering systemic roots, exploring unfamiliar technical domains, and mapping empirical boundary conditions.

9. **The Question Behind the Question: Resistance to Preventive Healthcare** (`cur-question-behind`, Beginner, 15-20 min)
   - *Dimensions:* Question Formulation, Exploration, Investigative Reasoning.
   - *Scenario:* 64% of young adults skip preventive checkups, citing "I feel fine," while underlying medical mistrust, billing opacity, and health anxiety persist.
   - *Deliverables:* Surface vs deep drivers, 4-level progressive question hierarchy, testable research hypothesis.

10. **Unexpected Connections: Mycelium Networks & Mesh Telecommunications** (`cur-unexpected-connections`, Intermediate, 25-30 min)
    - *Dimensions:* Exploration, Intellectual Openness, Information Seeking.
    - *Scenario:* Fungal networks allocate subterranean nutrients without a central brain; ad-hoc disaster Wi-Fi mesh networks struggle with node dropouts.
    - *Deliverables:* Three structural parallels, cross-disciplinary computational protocol invention.

11. **Deconstructing Common Wisdom: The '10,000-Hour Rule' Boundary Map** (`cur-deconstructing-wisdom`, Advanced, 30-35 min)
    - *Dimensions:* Investigative Reasoning, Information Seeking, Intellectual Openness.
    - *Scenario:* The viral popularization of Anders Ericsson's violin study into an ironclad universal recipe for world-class mastery.
    - *Deliverables:* Distortion audit, domain boundary map (Kind vs Wicked environments), epistemic humility lesson.

12. **Explore the Unknown: Cryptographic Zero-Knowledge Proofs** (`cur-explore-unknown`, Intermediate, 25-30 min)
    - *Dimensions:* Exploration, Question Formulation, Intellectual Openness.
    - *Scenario:* Proving you know a secret without revealing any information about the secret itself.
    - *Deliverables:* Conceptual paradox resolution, original real-world analogy, societal implications for human agency and privacy.
